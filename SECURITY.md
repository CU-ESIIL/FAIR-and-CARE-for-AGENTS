# Security, disclosure, and governance reports

This repository currently contains no research dataset, credentials, or approved governed data. Do not introduce them through source files, prompts, logs, issues, tests, or CI artifacts.

For a non-sensitive error or concern, open a GitHub issue. If a report would reveal credentials, personal information, governed knowledge, a sensitive species location, or another disclosure risk, contact the repository owner through an approved private institutional channel rather than a public issue. The public contact route and accountable owner are recorded in `project.json`.

Response and correction procedures are defined in `governance/INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md`. Publication and external transfer remain human-gated actions.
